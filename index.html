Our story

Two friends, one neighborhood.

We're Lucas and Ashton. We started Fresh Porch Co. to clean up the porches and walkways in our own neighborhood.

We began with a leaf blower, a couple of brooms and a pressure washer. We still work the same way: clear the debris, wash the surface, then finish by hand so it actually looks clean.

We're new, so every job matters to us. When you call, you'll talk to one of us, and one of us will be the one at your house.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to be home?

Usually not. We just need to be able to use an outdoor faucet and hose.

Will it hurt my plants or lawn?

No. We use gentle cleaners and keep the runoff away from your plants.

What if something doesn't look right?

Tell us when we finish, or contact us afterward. We'll take another look.

How do I pay?

We'll let you know when you book.
