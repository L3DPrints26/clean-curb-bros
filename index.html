Questions

Do I need to be home?

Nope, not usually. Leave the cans out after pickup. We just need to use your outdoor faucet and hose.

How do you clean them?

We rinse them out, scrub them with a cleaner, and deodorize them. Then we put them back and they air dry.

Will it hurt my plants or lawn?

No. We use gentle cleaners and keep the runoff away from your plants.

What if something doesn't look right?

Tell us, either before we leave or after. We'll come back and take a look.

How do I pay?

We'll let you know when you book.
