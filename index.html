FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to be home?

Nope, not usually. For trash cans, just leave them out after pickup. We only need to use your outdoor faucet and hose.

How do you clean the trash cans?

We rinse them out, scrub them with a cleaner, and deodorize them. Then we let them dry before we put them back.

Will it hurt my plants or lawn?

No. We use gentle cleaners and keep the runoff away from your plants.

What if something doesn't look right?

Just tell us, either before we leave or after. We'll come back and take a look.

How do I pay?

We'll let you know when you book.
